How long things take

Voting delay
1 minute
Voting period
10 minutes
Timelock delay
5 minutes

Voting delay: time between creating a proposal and the start of voting. Voting period: how long votes are accepted. Timelock delay: a safety wait between a passed vote and the moment it runs, so nobody is surprised.

Demo timings: they are compressed so a whole proposal fits in a short meeting. The production DAO uses 1 day to open, 7 days to vote, 48 hours of waiting.

How the DAO works

  1. 1

    Turn ATRM into votes

    Delegate your ATRM to yourself or to someone you trust. Undelegated ATRM does not vote.

  2. 2

    Propose

    Anyone with at least the threshold of voting power can propose. Voting opens 1 minute later.

  3. 3

    Vote

    ATRM holders vote For, Against or Abstain for 10 minutes. It passes with more For than Against and enough ATRM taking part (the quorum).

  4. 4

    Queue and execute

    A passed proposal waits 5 minutes in the timelock, a safety delay. Then anyone can execute it and the treasury acts.

By design, the DAO treasury only moves through a passed vote. Nothing on this page is financial advice or a promise of return.
